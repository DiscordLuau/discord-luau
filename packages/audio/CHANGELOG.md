## [Unreleased]

### Added

- `options.bitrate` on `audio.fromPCM`, `audio.fromFile`, `audio.fromEncoded` and the named sources sets the Opus bitrate (128000 by default, a whole number from 500 to 510000), checked at the call with `opus.checkBitrate`. Every encoder a source creates now also gets the `"music"` signal hint, and sources hold `bitrate`. The default raises PCM sources from the encoder's automatic bitrate, about 98 kbps for noisy music, to 128 kbps
- `audio.player.new(connection, options?)` builds a player for one voice connection. `player:playAsync(source)` sends Speaking, then each packet paced by its own duration, then five silence frames and Speaking 0, and resolves `"finished"`, or `"stopped"` after `player:stop()`. It fails with the close reason on a closed connection, with the send's error, with `audio source failed: <error>` for a raising source and with `invalid Opus packet from the audio source: <error>`, after a best-effort silence tail when Speaking went out. One player per connection plays at a time: a second `playAsync`, on the same player or another player of the connection, fails at once and leaves the running playback alone
- `audio.fromOpus(packets)` builds a single-use source from an array of Opus packets or a function that returns the next one, and `audio.fromPCM(pcm)` from 48 kHz stereo 16-bit little-endian PCM of any length (one buffer, an array of buffers or a function), cut into 20 ms frames, zero-padded at the end and encoded with one Opus encoder made on the first frame
- `audio.fromFile(path, options?)` streams an audio file from the disk, and `audio.fromEncoded(bytes, options?)` plays one held in memory as a buffer or string, both decoded with the new `discord_luau/symphonia` dependency. MP1, MP2 and MP3, AAC-LC (mono or stereo), FLAC, Vorbis, PCM, ADPCM (mono or stereo) and ALAC are decoded and encoded to Opus as they play, with PCM, FLAC, Vorbis and ALAC mixed to stereo from any channel layout; single-stream Opus in Ogg, WebM, Matroska or MP4 is passed through as stored, without its pre-skip, end trimming or output gain. An open failure raises `could not open audio: <message>` at the call, and a decode failure fails the playback with `audio source failed: could not decode audio: <message>`
- `audio.fromMP3`, `fromOgg`, `fromWAV`, `fromFLAC`, `fromAAC`, `fromM4A`, `fromWebM`, `fromMKV`, `fromAIFF` and `fromCAF` take a file's bytes and are aliases of `audio.fromEncoded`; the format is found from the content, so any supported file plays through any of them
- `audio.loadFileDecoder()` loads libsymphonia at startup, so the first file source neither yields nor pays the download and checksum cost
- `source:destroy()` frees a file source's decoder session and open file, even after the source has ended, and a raising release is warned rather than raised. A source not handed to a `playAsync` that starts must be destroyed
- `audio.fromOpus` given a buffer or string raises with a pointer to `audio.fromOgg` and `audio.fromFile`, and `audio.fromEncoded`, the named sources and `audio.fromFile` raise for an input of the wrong type with a pointer to the other kind of file source
- The player paces packets on an absolute schedule, `start + samples sent / 48` milliseconds, read from `options.clock` (`{ nowMillis, wait }`), whose default reads `std_polyfills`' `datetime.now` and `task.wait` at call time. Up to 100 ms behind it catches up; further behind, or when the wall clock steps back, it resets the schedule (logged at debug), so a stall never bursts and audio is never dropped
- While the voice connection resumes or rejoins, a playback waits, woken by the connection's `onReady`, `onResumed` and `onClosed`; sends lost to the reconnect are logged at debug and not sent again. On each new session it waits up to `mediaWaitSeconds` (5 by default) for DAVE media readiness, logs at info and plays on when the wait times out, and sends Speaking again. A close fails it with `voice connection closed: <closeDetail>`, and `stop` ends it at once from the readiness wait and when the media wait ends
- A README covering the sources, the player, its results and options, and a voice guide (`docs/guides/voice.md`) for joining, receiving, playing and DAVE
- `options.volume` on `audio.fromFile`, `audio.fromEncoded` and the named sources (1 by default, a number from 0 to 4, checked at the call), `source:setVolume(volume)` and `source.volume` set the volume of a file source's decoded audio. A change is heard at the next 100 ms read of the file, and audio that would pass full scale is limited rather than clipped. Opus passed through is unaffected, and `fromPCM` and `fromOpus` sources have no volume
- `audio.fromStream(read, options?)` plays an audio file that arrives in pieces: `read` returns the next chunk of its bytes, and may yield, and nil at the end. The call only checks `volume`, `bitrate`, `bufferSeconds` (1 by default, from 0.1 to 4) and `logLevel`; the playback's first poll opens a `discord_luau/symphonia` stream session and starts a pump thread that pushes the bytes in slices of at most 64 KiB. Decoded audio is read ahead up to 6 seconds before any volume, so `source:setVolume` on a stream is heard at the next packet, through the source's own limiter. The session, the pump and the queue belong to the source's `feed` (`source.feed.bufferedSeconds` says how much is buffered). Failures end the playback with `audio source failed: audio stream read failed: <error>`, `could not open audio: <message>`, `could not open audio: the stream ended before any audio`, `could not decode audio: <message>`, `invalid Opus packet from the audio source: <error>` or, for an error the pump did not expect, `the audio stream's pump failed: <error>`. A failure before the stream opens ends it with no Speaking, once the session has decided whether the bytes it already has open (at most 500 ms later); if they do, or the failure comes after the stream opened, the audio that came before it plays first, then the playback fails. A stream that ends early, such as a WAV followed by other bytes, stops being read. `source:destroy()` stops the pump without waiting for it
- `audio.fromProcess(command, arguments, options?)` plays what a program writes to stdout, such as `yt-dlp -f 251 -o - <url>`, as a stream source with the same options. The program starts at the playback's first poll; one that cannot start fails it with `could not start <command>: <reason>` and no Speaking. Stdout's 1 KiB reads are joined into chunks of up to 64 KiB without ever waiting on a slow program, and a read that raises fails the playback with `audio stream read failed: <error>` after the audio before it. Once stdout ends the stream waits up to 5 seconds for the exit code: a non-zero code wins over every other outcome (`<command> exited with code <n>: <last stderr line>`, read once stderr has ended, waiting up to 250 ms for it with a warning past that, or `no error output`), and a program that does not exit is killed, with a warning, and the stream ends normally. The program is killed only while its stdout is still open, at once when the stream fails while waiting on it, or at `destroy`, and a code that follows that kill is ignored. A kill never waits for the program: its stdout and stderr are closed, so one blocked on writing them exits even if it ignores SIGTERM, and `stop` and `destroy` return at once
- Stream sources play an MP4 or M4A with its index at the end, and a CAF, which cannot be read from the front: until the stream opens they keep its bytes, up to 128 MiB, and on that failure read the rest, yielding after every read, and play the whole file through `discord_luau/symphonia`'s `fromBuffer`, with memory up to about 4 times the file. A larger file fails with `could not open audio: this file must be complete before it plays and is larger than 128 MiB`
- The README and the voice guide cover streams: the `yt-dlp` route, `fromStream` with a Luau function, buffering and pauses, volume and bitrate, the whole-file fallback and its memory, and the YouTube tip of keeping the original Opus
- A Playing Music guide (`docs/guides/playing-music.md`) walks through playing a file, downloading a song and streaming one with yt-dlp, volume, and queues
- The player waits while a stream source is `starved`: before `bufferSeconds` of audio is buffered it sends nothing, and when a stream stalls it sends the silence tail and Speaking 0, polls the source every 20 ms, and speaks again on a fresh schedule once enough audio is buffered. `stop` ends that wait within about one 20 ms poll, and a source destroyed during it ends the playback without speaking. A source's `read` that returns nil while the source is `starved` now means "no data yet" rather than the end; sources other than streams are never starved
